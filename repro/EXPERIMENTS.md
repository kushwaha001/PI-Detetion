# Experiments: question, code, outputs, result

Regret = AUROC of the best layer minus AUROC of the chosen layer (lower is better).
"ID rule" = near-top peak of the intrinsic-dimension curve (eps = 0.1), frozen in `results_sweep\FROZEN_RULES.md`
after Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B; TinyLlama, OLMo-2, Qwen-3B and Qwen-7B are held-out tests of it.
